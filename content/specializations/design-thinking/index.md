This specialization guides students through the foundational principles of design thinking and human-centered design practice. Drawing on the double diamond framework, students progress through discovery, definition, development, and delivery — learning to see design as both a process and a mindset.

## Lessons

What is Design, Visual and Interaction Design, Storytelling, Open Design, Self Design
